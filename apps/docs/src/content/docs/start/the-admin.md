---
title: Finding your way around
description: What each part of the sidebar is, and how the admin is organised.
---

The admin is one sidebar and one screen. Every screen has its own address, so you can bookmark a
page you edit often and share a link to it with a colleague.

## The sidebar

**Content** — one entry per kind of content your site has: Pages, Events, News, whatever was set
up. Each is its own destination rather than a filter on a shared list, because they are genuinely
different places.

At the bottom of that group:

- **All content** — searches across every type at once. Useful when you know a title but not what
  kind of thing it is.
- **Releases** — batches of content staged to go live together. See
  [Releases](/publishing/releases/).

**Singletons** — appears only if your site has any. A singleton is a piece of content there is
exactly one of: a site-wide announcement banner, footer contact details. There is no list, so
clicking one opens its editor directly.

**Library**

- **Media** — every image and file that has been uploaded.
- **Reusable blocks** — pieces of content shared across several pages, edited in one place.
- **Taxonomies** — the tag and category vocabularies your site uses.
- **Menus** — the site's navigation. Administrators only.

**Settings** — administrators only. Content types, block types, redirects, people, API keys, the
audit log, and system information.

## Things that are the same on every screen

**Status badges** always carry their label as text, not just a colour, and a given colour means the
same thing everywhere. Learning them once is enough.

**Timestamps** shorten as they get closer — a time for today, a date for this year, a date with a
year for anything older. Hover for the exact moment.

**Breadcrumbs** at the top of a screen are links. They are the fastest way back up.

**Delete lives at the bottom of an editor**, under its own heading, and asks you to type something
to confirm. If a delete would break something, Taproot lists what to clear first instead of offering
the button.

## Light and dark

At the bottom of the sidebar. **System** follows your computer's setting and is the default; Light
and Dark override it. The choice is remembered per browser.
